## About project
This Python script calculates kinetic energy, potential energy and total energy when mass, velocity and height is entered

## Example
Mass: 5kg
Velocity: 10 m/s
Height 2 m
gravitational acceleration (g) = 9.8 (meters per square second)

Output: 
- Kinetic Energy: `1/2 * Mass * (Velocity ** 2) = 1/2 * 5 * (10 ** 2) = 250 J`.
- Potential Energy: `Mass * gravitational acceleration (g) * height = 5 * 9.8 * 2 = 98 J`.
- Total Energy: `Kinetic Energy + Potential Energy = 250J + 98J = 348 J`.
